# Experience assets

- `td-logo.svg` is the Toronto-Dominion Bank logo sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toronto-Dominion_Bank_logo.svg). The TD name and logo remain trademarks of The Toronto-Dominion Bank.
- `uwaterloo-logo.png` is the official horizontal colour logo from the [University of Waterloo brand library](https://uwaterloo.ca/brand/uw-logos).

## Photo slots

Add the two landscape TD photos and two portrait Waterloo photos to this folder. Then change the matching placeholder objects in `src/data/experience.ts` and `src/data/education.ts` to `kind: "image"` and add their `/images/experience/...` paths as `src` values.
